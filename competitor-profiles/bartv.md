## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: All the major sports for your bar without tying yourself to any operator — and you install it yourself in minutes.

**Target audience**: Bar owners across Spain of all sizes. Particularly effective pitch for those burned by telco contracts or in rural areas with limited connectivity options.

**Positioning angle**: Pure-play OTT sports for bars. No telco baggage, no permanence, maximum flexibility.

**Key messaging themes**:
- Widest sports catalogue (LaLiga 1+2, UCL, UEL, UECL, F1, MotoGP, Premier, Eurosport, GOL) — homepage
- Zero commitment — homepage
- Self-installation simplicity — product page
- Works with any internet provider — product page

## Competitive Implications for +BarSportTV

**Where BarTV is strong vs. +BarSportTV**:
- Wider content catalogue (Eurosport, GOL)
- Self-install is genuinely simpler for tech-averse bar owners
- No brand/beer affiliation (some bar owners may prefer a neutral provider)

**Where +BarSportTV is strong vs. BarTV**:
- Beer delivery bundle — unique, creates stickiness
- Mahou brand trust in hostelería (existing commercial relationship)
- Broader +Bar platform (equipment, consulting, management services)
- 15,000+ social proof claim (BarTV has no public equivalent)

**Opportunities**:
- Add Eurosport and GOL to content catalogue to match BarTV breadth
- Emphasise configured (not self-install) decoder as a service benefit, not a weakness
- Leverage Mahou's sales force and existing bar relationships for distribution

**Threats**:
- BarTV is structurally well-positioned if LaLiga tightens OTT rights distribution
- Self-install will become more expected as the market matures
