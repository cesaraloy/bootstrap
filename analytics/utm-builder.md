## UTM Rules

1. **Always lowercase** — `Instagram` breaks attribution, `instagram` works
2. **Underscores not hyphens** — consistent across all parameters
3. **utm_content identifies the specific post** — format: `[format]_[descriptor]`
   - Formats: `reel_`, `carousel_`, `story_`, `static_`
4. **Never reuse utm_content values** — each post should have a unique identifier
5. **Document every link** — add to the tracking spreadsheet when published
